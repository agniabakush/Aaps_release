## Reading this


The primary contrast is one-minute against the same sensor used at five minutes, so both arms run inside the same sensor sessions and the days above are days of trial, not days of wear. Each calendar day of the phase contributes to one arm, so a phase of N days yields about N/2 days per arm.


The TBR figures are here to size the SAFETY comparison, not to power a benefit claim. The stopping rules do not wait for significance: they are absolute thresholds and they bind whatever this table says.
